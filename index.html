I'm Sail, a CTE educator and tech enthusiast teaching Cisco Networking, French, Drafting, and Artificial Intelligence. My classrooms are hands-on, project-based, and deeply connected to the real world.

Beyond teaching, I design educational technology that closes the gap between what students need and what schools can provide. I founded AI-Ready Educator to help fellow teachers cut through the hype and actually use AI to save time and improve outcomes.

My most ambitious project, WildCatalyze AI, is an adaptive tutoring system that gives every math student a personalized learning path — built with the same care I bring to every classroom.
